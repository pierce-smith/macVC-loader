# code of conduct

small modding project, so this is short. be someone people actually want to make mods with.

- be patient with people new to modding, macs or programming. everybody started somewhere
- go after the code or the idea, not the person. say why, and suggest something better
- give credit: original mod authors, whoever found the offset, whoever tested it
- assume good faith. text loses tone, ask before you get offended

not ok:
- harassment, insults, slurs, going after anyone for who they are
- posting pirated game files or links to them, or other people's mods without permission
- posting someone's private info
- keeping it up after someone asked you to stop

if something's wrong, contact me through the project page (privately is fine). i can remove comments, close issues or ban people who don't follow this, with or without a warning depending on how bad it is.
